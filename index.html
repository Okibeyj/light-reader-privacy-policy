By installing or using the Application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Purpose and Functionality of the Application

Light Reader Pro is designed as a reading tool. It does not operate its own content-hosting platform or maintain a library of books for distribution.

The Application allows users to access and read content through features such as:

  • Local EPUB Files: Users can open EPUB documents stored on their mobile devices.
  • Third-Party Reading Sources and Websites: Users can access external websites and reading sources through the Application.
  • Offline Reading: Users can save supported reading content on their devices for access without an internet connection.

The Service Provider does not claim ownership of content available through external websites or sources. Users are responsible for ensuring that their access to and use of such content complies with applicable laws and the rights of the relevant content owners.

Imported reading files, saved bookmarks, reading progress, and supported downloaded content are stored locally on the user's device. The Service Provider does not receive the contents of these materials through the Application.

When you access a third-party website or reading source, your device communicates with that external service. The website or service may independently receive technical information, such as your IP address, and may use cookies or other technologies under its own privacy policy. The Service Provider does not control the information-handling practices of those external services.

3. Information Collection and Usage

The Application does not require users to create an account or submit personal information to use its reading features.

The Service Provider does not use the Application to collect personal information, track individual reading activity, or collect analytics and usage statistics. The Application does not use advertising or analytics SDKs for these purposes.

Reading information such as imported files, bookmarks, saved reading progress, and supported downloaded content is stored locally on your device.

When you choose to access an external website or reading source, that third party may process information necessary to provide its website or service. Such processing is governed by the third party's own privacy policy.

4. Sharing Information with Third Parties

The Service Provider does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information collected through the Application because the Application is not designed to collect such information.

External websites and reading sources accessed through the Application operate independently. Any information your device sends to those services is handled according to their respective privacy policies and practices.

5. Uninstalling the Application

You may stop using Light Reader Pro at any time by uninstalling it through your device's standard application management settings.

Uninstalling the Application removes its locally stored application data according to your device's operating system and settings. Any information independently retained by third-party websites or services is subject to their own data retention and deletion policies.
